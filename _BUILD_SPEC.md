## 2. DEPTH STANDARD (per playbook §0 — first pass, no thin pages)

- **Service pages: 900–1,400 words.** Structure: answer-first intro (2–3 sentences that directly
  answer "what is this service / do I need it") → signs you need it → what's included → step-by-step
  process → materials/options → what it costs (ranges from `_RESEARCH.md` / cost page, cited) → why
  hire a local pro → related services + area links → **5–7 FAQs** with matching FAQPage schema.
- **Area pages: 800–1,200 words.** Structure: answer-first intro naming the town → local housing
  stock and deck age (use the verified stats) → terrain/climate issues specific to that town →
  services we do there → **county permit rules for THAT county** (Baltimore Co. vs Howard vs Anne
  Arundel vs Harford vs Carroll vs City — they differ, that's the value) → named neighborhoods/roads
  /landmarks from `_RESEARCH.md` → **5 local FAQs** + schema.
- **Blog posts: 1,000–1,600 words**, answer-first, heavy on verified specifics, FAQ schema where a
  Q&A section exists.
- **Cost page: 1,200+ words**, real cited ranges, per-item table, what drives price up/down. No
  competitor publishes pricing — this page is a wedge. Cite sources; mark national figures national.

### AEO requirements (every page)
- Open every page and every major section with a **direct 1–2 sentence answer**, then expand.
- Question-shaped H2/H3s using real homeowner phrasing.
- Facts in lists/steps/tables, not buried in prose.
- FAQ JSON-LD mirrors the visible FAQ exactly.
